# CertaNest — Brand Guidelines (Foundation)

> **Status:** Approved. CertaNest is the official brand (formerly DueNest), and
> this foundation is being integrated across the product.

## Brand name

**CertaNest** — always written as one word, capital **C** and capital **N**.

Correct: `CertaNest`
Incorrect: `Certanest`, `certaNest`, `Certa Nest`, `CERTANEST` (except where an
all-caps style is used consistently for short UI labels), `Certa-Nest`.

## Meaning

CertaNest = **certainty + nest**.

It should feel like a **secure home for important life documents** — deadlines,
proof, renewals, subscriptions, application packs, trusted sharing, and emergency
access. The "nest" is calm, protective, and organized. The "certainty" is the
relief of knowing everything important is ready when life asks.

## Core positioning

CertaNest is a **secure life-admin platform** that keeps important documents,
deadlines, renewals, subscriptions, and reusable application packs organized and
ready whenever life asks.

It is **not** a generic cloud drive, generic PDF tool, finance/budgeting app, a
plain subscription tracker, a job board, a social app, or an enterprise
e-signature clone. CertaNest stays focused on **life-admin readiness**.

## Taglines

- **Main tagline:** Life documents, deadlines, and proof — ready when life asks.
- **Short tagline:** Your life-admin, securely organized.

## Brand personality

Secure · calm · premium · organized · trustworthy · modern · human · slightly warm.

| We are | We are not |
| --- | --- |
| Calm and reassuring | Anxious or alarmist |
| Premium and considered | Cheap or cluttered |
| Warm and human | Cold or corporate |
| Secure and precise | Vague or careless |
| Organized | Busy or noisy |

## What to avoid (visual)

- ❌ School / graduation certificate imagery
- ❌ Literal birds, eggs, or twiggy nests
- ❌ Government seals or official-looking stamps
- ❌ Generic shields or basic padlock icons
- ❌ Messy file stacks or paper piles
- ❌ Childish illustrations or playful mascots
- ❌ Heavy gradients, random animation, or neon

CertaNest's security cues come from **structure, calm spacing, and rounded
protective forms** — not from clichéd "security" iconography.
