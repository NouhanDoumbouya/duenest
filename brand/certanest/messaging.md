# CertaNest — Messaging

> Foundation only. Copy for review; not yet published.

## Name & meaning

**CertaNest** — certainty + nest. A secure home where important life-admin is kept
ready: documents, deadlines, proof, renewals, subscriptions, application packs,
trusted sharing, and emergency access.

## Taglines

- **Main:** Life documents, deadlines, and proof — ready when life asks.
- **Short:** Your life-admin, securely organized.

## One-line positioning

CertaNest is a secure life-admin platform that keeps important documents,
deadlines, renewals, subscriptions, and reusable application packs organized and
ready whenever life asks.

## Elevator (50 words)

Life-admin is scattered across drives, inboxes, and drawers — and it always
resurfaces at the worst moment. CertaNest is a secure nest for the documents,
deadlines, renewals, and proof that life keeps asking for, so when a deadline,
application, renewal, or emergency arrives, everything important is already ready.

## Value pillars

1. **Organized** — Every important document in one calm, secure place.
2. **Ready** — Deadlines, renewals, and proof tracked before they bite.
3. **Reusable** — Application packs you build once and reuse for life's forms.
4. **Trusted sharing** — Share safely, with control, and revoke when done.
5. **Ready for emergencies** — The right people can reach the right documents when it matters.

## Voice & tone

- **Calm, not anxious.** We reduce worry; we never manufacture it.
- **Clear, not clever.** Plain words about important things.
- **Warm, not cute.** Human and reassuring, never childish.
- **Precise, not corporate.** Trustworthy specifics over buzzwords.

## Trust copy (reusable lines)

- Private until you share it.
- No public link has been created yet.
- Your original file is always preserved.
- Review before you share.
- You can revoke access at any time.
- Stored securely. Visible only to you, until you choose otherwise.

## Words we prefer / avoid

| Prefer | Avoid |
| --- | --- |
| ready, prepared, organized, secure, proof | guaranteed, foolproof, bulletproof |
| life-admin, important documents | files, stuff, paperwork (dismissive) |
| deadlines, renewals | due dates only (too narrow) |
| share safely, trusted sharing | send (implies loss of control) |
