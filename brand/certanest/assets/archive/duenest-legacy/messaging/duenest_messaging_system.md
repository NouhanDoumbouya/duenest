# DueNest Messaging System

## Primary positioning
DueNest is an AI-powered life-admin workspace that helps people manage essential documents, deadlines, subscriptions, renewals, and reusable application packs in one secure place.

## Primary tagline
Documents, deadlines, and renewals in one secure AI workspace.

## Short tagline options
- Never miss what matters.
- Your documents. Your deadlines. Organized.
- The secure nest for life's important deadlines.
- Keep documents ready and deadlines under control.

## 10-second pitch
DueNest helps students, professionals, freelancers, families, and small teams store essential documents, track expiry dates and renewals, and generate ready-to-use application document packs.

## 30-second pitch
DueNest is a secure life-admin platform for managing important documents, subscriptions, renewals, and deadlines. Users can upload documents such as passports, visas, certificates, contracts, receipts, and insurance files, then receive smart reminders before expiry or renewal dates. Later, AI extraction and application packs help users prepare for jobs, scholarships, visas, internships, and business compliance without searching through scattered folders.

## Target users
- International students and immigrants
- Job seekers and scholarship applicants
- Freelancers and creators
- Families managing shared documents
- Small teams and NGOs managing renewals and compliance

## Brand voice
Clear, trustworthy, calm, intelligent, protective, and practical. Avoid fear-based language. Focus on peace of mind, readiness, and control.
