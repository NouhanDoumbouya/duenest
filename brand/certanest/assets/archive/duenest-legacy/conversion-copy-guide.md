# DueNest — Conversion Copy Guide

Goal: a visitor understands DueNest in 5–10 seconds and knows the next action.

## Hero

- **Headline:** Important documents, ready when life asks.
  *(Alt: Where important documents become ready.)*
- **Subhead:** DueNest helps you scan, organize, prepare, track, generate, and
  safely share important documents before deadlines, applications, renewals, and
  emergencies.
- **Primary CTA:** Start organizing for free
- **Secondary CTA:** See how it works
- **Hero trust line:** Private until shared. No public link without your confirmation.

## Landing section order

1. Hero → 2. Problem → 3. Core workflow → 4. Main features → 5. Use cases →
6. AI assistance → 7. Safe sharing → 8. Application packs → 9. For individuals →
10. For organizations / portals → 11. Trust & privacy → 12. Pricing teaser →
13. Final CTA.

## CTAs

**Primary (action-specific):** Start organizing for free · Upload your first
document · Scan your first document · Create an application pack · Track a
deadline · Share safely.

**Secondary:** See how it works · Explore features · Prepare a pack · Try AI
Assistant · Create your Vault.

**Avoid:** "Learn more", bare "Get started" everywhere, "Click here".

**Rules:** match CTA to user intent; make the first action obvious; on mobile the
primary CTA must be visible without clutter.

## Onboarding (goal-based)

First question: **What do you want to prepare first?** Options route to the
relevant first action with a short explanation and a skip option:
- Organize my important documents
- Prepare an application pack
- Track a document deadline
- Scan a document
- Share documents safely
- Use AI with my documents
- Set up emergency access

Reassurance copy: "Start with one document." · "You can organize more later." ·
"No public link will be created unless you confirm." · "AI suggestions are
optional." · "Your original files are preserved."

## Trust copy (only when behavior is real)

Private until shared · Original document preserved · Removed from pack. File not
deleted. · Moved to Trash. You can restore it. · Permanent delete cannot be
undone. · AI suggestions need your review. · This QR follows your SafeSend access
rules. · Requirements vary. Verify with the official source. · This tool helps
prepare a signed copy. Legal acceptance may depend on recipient and jurisdiction.

## Honesty

No fake testimonials/numbers/awards/certifications. No "legally binding" or
"guaranteed accepted". No official-requirement or unlimited-infrastructure claims.
