# DueNest — Voice & Tone

DueNest handles sensitive, important documents. The voice must earn trust.

## Voice (always)

Calm · clear · trustworthy · practical · confident · human · precise ·
privacy-first.

**Not:** childish, corporate-heavy, fear-based, hype-y, or noisy.

## Tone by situation

| Context | Tone |
|---|---|
| Landing page | Confident, clear, benefit-driven |
| Dashboard | Helpful, concise, action-oriented |
| Vault | Calm, organized, reassuring |
| Delete / Trash | Careful, explicit, recoverable |
| SafeSend | Trustworthy, unambiguous |
| AI | Helpful, honest, review-first |
| Emergency | Serious, minimal, calm |
| Errors | Reassuring, recovery-focused |

## Good examples

- "No public link has been created yet."
- "Original document preserved."
- "Moved to Trash. Undo."
- "Review AI suggestions before saving."
- "Requirements vary. Always verify with the official source."
- "This QR follows your SafeSend access rules."
- "Permanent delete cannot be undone."

## Avoid

- "Magically manage everything."
- "Guaranteed accepted." / "100% ATS pass."
- "Legally binding signature."
- "Your documents are perfectly ready."
- "AI knows what you need."

## Writing rules

- One sentence should explain any major page.
- Every risky action explains its consequence.
- Every success state offers a clear next action.
- Prefer verbs over nouns; short over clever.
- Never show trust copy for behavior that isn't implemented.
