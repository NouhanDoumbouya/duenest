## Students (`/use-cases/students`)

- **Pain:** Academic, visa, scholarship, and job documents are scattered across
  phone, email, WhatsApp, and Drive — and something always expires at the worst time.
- **Solution:** Keep every important document ready in one private Vault, with
  reminders before anything expires.
- **Workflow:** Scan/upload → organize in Vault → track deadlines → build
  application packs → share safely.
- **Features:** Vault, Scan, Application Packs, Deadlines & Renewals, Document
  Generation, SafeSend.
- **CTA:** Start organizing for free.
- **Trust:** Private until shared. Your originals are preserved.

## Visa documents (`/use-cases/visa-documents`)

- **Pain:** A missing or expired document can delay or derail a visa application.
- **Solution:** Prepare a complete visa pack, track expiry dates, and share safely.
- **Workflow:** Build a visa pack → attach/scan documents → set passport/visa
  reminders → fill & sign where needed → SafeSend with expiry + QR.
- **Features:** Application Packs (visa), Deadlines & Renewals, Fill & Sign,
  SafeSend, Custom QR.
- **CTA:** Prepare a visa pack.
- **Trust:** Requirements vary — always verify with the official source.

## Scholarship applications (`/use-cases/scholarship-applications`)

- **Pain:** Scholarship packs come together last-minute and incomplete.
- **Solution:** Build a complete scholarship pack with a checklist and document drafts.
- **Workflow:** Choose scholarship pack → checklist of required/optional items →
  attach documents → generate statements/letters → track the deadline.
- **Features:** Application Packs (scholarship), Document Generation, Deadlines,
  SafeSend.
- **CTA:** Create an application pack.
- **Trust:** Templates are generic and editable; verify requirements with the source.

## Job applications (`/use-cases/job-applications`)

- **Pain:** Every role needs a tailored CV, cover letter, certificates, and its
  own deadline.
- **Solution:** Build a job application pack with drafts, supporting documents,
  and deadline tracking.
- **Workflow:** Create job pack → generate CV/cover-letter drafts (review-first)
  → attach certificates → track deadline → export/share.
- **Features:** Application Packs (job), Document Generation, Deadlines, Document
  Tools, SafeSend.
- **CTA:** Create a job pack.
- **Trust:** Drafts are starting points you edit. No "guaranteed accepted" claims.

## Document sharing (`/use-cases/document-sharing`)

- **Pain:** Sensitive documents get shared over email/WhatsApp and can't be taken back.
- **Solution:** Share with expiry, access rules, QR, and revocation — never a
  public link by accident.
- **Workflow:** Select document/pack → SafeSend → set expiry/access → confirm →
  share link or Custom QR → revoke anytime.
- **Features:** SafeSend, Custom QR, prepared copies, Document Tools.
- **CTA:** Share safely.
- **Trust:** No public link is created without your confirmation. Revoke anytime.

## Agencies & schools (`/use-cases/agencies-schools`)

- **Pain:** Collecting documents from many applicants/clients/students is chaotic.
- **Solution:** Request, review, track, and safely manage submissions in one place.
- **Workflow:** Create a portal → request specific documents via secure upload
  links → review status → send reminders → organize received documents.
- **Features:** DueNest Portals, Document Requests, checklists, Deadlines, SafeSend,
  Organizations workspace.
- **CTA:** Create a portal.
- **Trust:** Upload links are scoped; recipients never see your workspace.
